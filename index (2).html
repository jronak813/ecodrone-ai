    Every scan is saved on this device. Reports not yet sent are marked so nothing is missed.

    How to read the results: the app looks for plastic-like colours (blue, white, red, yellow) and shapes. Big connected patches count as macroplastic; tiny separate specks count as microplastic. A drone photo cannot see plastic smaller than a few millimetres, so “microplastic” here is a screening estimate; a UV photo and the sensors make it more reliable. Flowers, foam or sunlight on water can look like plastic, so always confirm on site.
